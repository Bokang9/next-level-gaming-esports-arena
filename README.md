# Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena

A modern, gaming-themed website for a fictional esports and gaming venue based in Johannesburg. The website introduces the business, highlights the main gaming experiences and packages, helps users estimate prices, and allows visitors to prepare an enquiry.

## Project purpose

The purpose of this website is to give customers an easy way to:
- learn about the business
- explore gaming services and experiences
- understand package options
- estimate the cost of a session
- contact the venue for more information or booking enquiries

The website is designed to look modern and exciting, matching the gaming and esports vibe of the business.

---

## Website explanation

### 1. Home page
The Home page is the first page visitors see. It introduces the business and contains:
- the brand name and logo
- a short promotional message
- navigation links to all pages
- call-to-action buttons

The main buttons allow users to:
- view the different experiences
- calculate their fees
- continue into the booking process

The page also includes feature cards that present:
- PC gaming
- console gaming
- group sessions

These cards help users understand what the business offers immediately.

### 2. About Us page
This page tells the story of the venue. It explains:
- when the business was created
- who founded it
- what the business aims to provide
- why the arena is important for gaming and esports

The page helps build trust and gives the business a personality. It explains that the venue is designed for:
- casual players
- families and friends
- schools
- businesses
- esports competitions and events

### 3. Experiences page
The Experiences page lists individual activities and gaming sessions. The examples include:
- PC Gaming Session
- Console Gaming
- Group Gaming

Each card includes:
- a title
- a short description
- an example price
- a button to view more information or continue

This page helps users compare the different activities and decide what they want to try.

### 4. Featured Package page
This is the page for the featured package. The example package is the PC Gaming Session.

The page contains:
- the package title
- a description
- the included features
- example pricing
- buttons leading to cost estimation or contact

This page is important because it gives visitors a deeper look at the service before they commit to a booking.

### 5. Calculate Fees page
This is one of the most important pages on the website because it allows users to estimate the cost of their session.

The page includes two calculators:

#### Hourly calculator
This calculator is used for:
- PC Gaming at R50 per player per hour
- Console Gaming at R40 per player per hour

Users enter:
- the gaming experience
- number of players
- number of hours

The page calculates:
- subtotal
- VAT
- final estimated total

#### Package and experience calculator
This calculator allows users to select various:
- packages
- individual experiences

It then:
- adds the chosen items together
- applies a discount depending on how many services are selected
- calculates VAT
- shows the final estimated total

The discount structure is:
- 1 booking: 0%
- 2 bookings: 5%
- 3 bookings: 10%
- 4+ bookings: 15%

The final total is only an estimate and is not an official invoice.

### 6. Contact page
The Contact page allows visitors to ask questions or request more information. It includes:
- contact information
- location
- email
- opening hours
- an enquiry form

The form asks for:
- name
- email
- phone number
- enquiry topic
- message

When the user submits the form successfully, a confirmation message appears. In this prototype, it is a front-end demonstration rather than a real backend email service.

## Design choices

The website uses a dark, futuristic look to match the gaming theme.

### Colour palette
- Dark navy/black backgrounds create a premium gaming atmosphere.
- Cyan and blue are used for headings, buttons and highlights.
- Green is used for positive actions such as booking and successful actions.
- Purple accents are used for decorative and brand elements.

### Layout style
- Big headings create strong visual focus
- Cards are used to group information clearly
- Buttons are large and easy to tap
- The layout is responsive for desktop and mobile screens
- White and light grey text keep content readable on dark backgrounds

## How the website works

A typical customer journey is:

1. Visit the Home page
2. Read about the gaming arena
3. Browse experiences or packages
4. Select one or more services
5. Calculate the estimated total
6. Contact the business to confirm availability

This makes the website useful for both information and conversion.
